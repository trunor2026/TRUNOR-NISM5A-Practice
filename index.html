Welcome to the TRUNOR Partner Guide

A simple step-by-step guide designed to help TRUNOR partners understand and complete important operational procedures quickly and correctly.

About TRUNOR

TRUNOR is a financial services company focused on helping individuals and families make informed financial decisions and work towards their long-term financial goals.

Our approach is centered around providing practical financial guidance and access to a range of investment, savings and protection solutions based on individual requirements.

What is the Partner Guide?

The TRUNOR Partner Guide is a centralized resource created to help partners understand and follow operational procedures step by step.

Instead of searching through different messages, screenshots or instructions, partners can use this guide to find the required procedure in one place.

1

Step-by-Step Instructions

3

Easy Navigation

Use the sidebar to quickly move between different procedures.

4

Quick Search

Search for a procedure or keyword using the search bar at the top.
