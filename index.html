How the arcade works. Every mission is a full-syllabus mock — 50 multiple-choice questions and a 10-question True/False round — at its own difficulty. You earn XP for every correct answer (harder missions pay more), and clearing a mission the first time pays a bonus. XP builds your level and rank. Come back daily to keep your streak alive. Progress is saved on this device only. The boss fight — the Full Mock — mixes 75 MCQs and 25 True/False under a live 120-minute timer, exactly like the real exam: 100 questions, 1 mark each, no negative marking, pass at 50%.

Accuracy note. Questions are built from the NISM Series V-A workbook (Dec 2025) and SEBI/AMFI rules current at the time of writing. Tax rates, TER limits and thresholds change — verify anything critical against the latest workbook before you rely on it. This is a practice aid, not official NISM material.
